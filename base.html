My Story

I started coding at age 14, building simple websites for local businesses in my hometown. What began as curiosity quickly turned into passion. After earning my Computer Science degree from Stanford, I joined a YC-backed startup where I led the frontend team through a complete product redesign.

Today, I work at the intersection of design and engineering — believing that the best products are born when aesthetics and functionality meet. I'm driven by the challenge of creating software that not only works flawlessly but feels delightful to use.

"My ambition is to build technology that empowers people — tools that are accessible, ethical, and genuinely make life better. I aim to lead a product team that ships with empathy and ships often."

Career Goals

Short-term, I'm focused on deepening my expertise in AI-driven interfaces and cloud architecture. Long-term, I aspire to become a CTO or founding engineer at a mission-driven startup, building products that solve real-world problems at scale.
